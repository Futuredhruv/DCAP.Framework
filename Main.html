Context: The Challenge of Coercive Control

Coercive control is a pattern of incidents used to exert power. Unlike physical violence, its evidence lies in subtle, longitudinal linguistic patterns within thousands of text messages. Current forensic methods rely on manual review or analyzing police summaries (secondary sources), missing the granular "primary source" evidence.

💡

Innovation: This framework introduces a "Human-in-the-Loop" (HITL) approach, using a forensically-optimized 9-trait AI model to flag potential abuse, empowering investigators rather than replacing them.

Implications & Ethics

Why this framework matters for the future of forensic science.

1

Forensic Triage Tool

Provides a "heatmap" of linguistic abuse, allowing units to prioritize high-risk cases (like the 20% flag rate in Case-001) over low-risk ones, optimizing resource allocation.

2

Objective Evidence

Moves beyond subjective "feeling" to quantifiable patterns. A prosecutor can present: "7.21% of messages showed Entitlement," providing a statistical backbone to the case.

Ethical & Legal Considerations

Not a Diagnostic Tool

DCAP detects linguistic markers aligned with DSM-5 traits, it does not diagnose NPD. It is a decision-support system, not a doctor.

Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)

To mitigate automation bias, the tool acts as a pointer. The human investigator must verify the flagged messages. The system ensures explainability (XAI) by linking every flag back to the source text.
